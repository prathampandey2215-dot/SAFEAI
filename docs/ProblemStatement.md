Problem Statement

Background

As the world becomes more connected, cyber threats are increasing rapidly. Millions of people use smartphones every day for banking, shopping, education, communication, and work. However, many users lack the knowledge and tools needed to identify online threats.

Cybercriminals exploit this gap through phishing websites, fake messages, scam phone calls, malicious applications, fraudulent QR codes, and social engineering attacks.

The Problem

Existing cybersecurity tools are often designed for technical users. They can be difficult to understand, expensive, or provide confusing warnings that ordinary users may ignore.

As a result, many people become victims of online fraud and lose money, personal information, or access to their accounts.

Our Solution

SafeAI aims to simplify cybersecurity by providing an AI-powered assistant that helps users recognize digital threats in clear, easy-to-understand language.

The application will analyze suspicious messages, websites, QR codes, and other potential threats, then explain the risks and recommend appropriate actions.

Objectives

- Protect users from common cyber threats.
- Make cybersecurity simple and accessible.
- Reduce phishing and online fraud.
- Increase cybersecurity awareness.
- Build trust through transparent AI assistance.

Expected Impact

SafeAI will help individuals make safer online decisions by providing intelligent guidance and practical security recommendations without requiring technical expertise.
