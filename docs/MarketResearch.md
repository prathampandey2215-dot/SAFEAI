Market Research

Introduction

The global cybersecurity market is growing rapidly as cyber threats become more common. Billions of people use smartphones every day, but many users have limited knowledge of online security. This creates a strong demand for simple and intelligent cybersecurity solutions.

---

Target Audience

Primary Users

- Students
- Families
- Senior Citizens
- Android Users
- Small Business Owners

Secondary Users

- Schools
- Colleges
- Freelancers
- Teachers
- Non-technical Professionals

---

Common Problems

Users frequently face:

- Phishing websites
- Fake QR codes
- Scam SMS messages
- Fraudulent emails
- Social engineering attacks
- Fake shopping websites
- Weak passwords
- Unsafe mobile applications

Many users cannot easily determine whether a website, message, or application is trustworthy.

---

Existing Solutions

Current cybersecurity products provide valuable protection but are often designed for technical users or enterprise environments.

Common categories include:

- Antivirus software
- Password managers
- VPN services
- Anti-phishing tools
- Mobile security applications

---

Market Gap

Many existing tools focus on detecting threats but do not explain them in a simple way that everyday users can understand.

There is an opportunity to build an AI-powered assistant that combines threat detection with clear guidance, making cybersecurity more accessible.

---

SafeAI Value Proposition

SafeAI aims to:

- Explain security risks in simple language.
- Help users identify suspicious content.
- Improve digital safety awareness.
- Provide an easy-to-use experience.
- Support users with AI-powered assistance.

---

Future Opportunities

Potential future expansion includes:

- Browser extensions
- Desktop applications
- Enterprise features
- Educational cybersecurity platform
- Multi-language support
- Family protection features

---

Conclusion

As digital threats continue to evolve, there is an increasing need for cybersecurity tools that are easy to understand and use. SafeAI aims to address this need by combining AI with user-friendly security guidance.
