# Risk Assessment

## Project

SafeAI – AI Powered Cybersecurity Platform

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document identifies, evaluates, and prioritizes potential risks that may affect the SafeAI project. It also defines mitigation strategies to reduce the likelihood and impact of those risks.

---

# 2. Risk Assessment Process

The project follows these steps:

1. Identify Risks
2. Analyze Risks
3. Evaluate Impact
4. Define Mitigation
5. Monitor Risks
6. Review Regularly

---

# 3. Risk Categories

The project considers the following categories:

- Technical Risks
- Security Risks
- Operational Risks
- Financial Risks
- Legal Risks
- AI Risks
- Infrastructure Risks
- Project Management Risks
